# g01 Roll-Up — Foundational and Research Generation

Status: archived
Generation: `g01` (`001`–`046`)
Succeeding generation: `g02` (active, closing)

## Intent and boundary

`g01` built Convergence from a local snap store through a central-authority
MVP, the gates/bundles/promotion model, superposition resolution, TUI
coverage, lanes and collaboration, identity, releases and retention — then
aligned the work with Northstar doctrine (042) and ran the Comparative
Research Program (043–045). It closed with the docs spine restructure
(2026-07-23), which moved its roadmap files to branch `archive/g01` and
handed the live queue to `g02` through the archive-and-rebuild boundary
(`g02.002`).

## Shipped outcomes

- **Snap store and central authority** (001–002): local snap capture,
  central MVP.
- **Gate model** (003, 032–033): bundles, promotion across gates,
  per-gate release policy, gate-graph administration.
- **Superposition and resolution semantics** (006–011): conflict preserved
  as data, resolution variants, validation, provenance.
- **TUI program** (004, 012–017, 023, 028–031, 035–041): command shell,
  remote browsers, snapshots view, hybrid views, modal modes, releases
  browser, simplification, prompted commands, module split, server
  bootstrap, second-nature hardening, remote guidance and onboarding.
- **Sync and collaboration** (005, 018–019): dev-server persistence,
  large-object storage, lanes with unpublished sync.
- **Identity and authentication** (020): the first identity seam.
- **Releases, retention, GC** (021–022, 025–027): release channels and
  diffs, operator releases, retention tooling, remote GC.
- **Hardening** (022, 024, 034–035): polish, rigorous testing, god-file
  decomposition and post-decomposition hardening.
- **Doctrine and research** (042–045): Northstar doctrine alignment,
  baseline specimen dossiers, core-value track syntheses, translation
  memos that fed the architecture. Optional expansion (046) remains
  lineage, not the live queue.

## Migrations, limits, retained risks

- Vocabulary moved on: `bundle` became `candidate` (`g02.029`); release
  `channels` were retired in favour of semver releases (`g02.028`). The
  g01 files keep their era terms.
- The g01 tree was abandoned by the rebuild (`g02.002`); anything that
  mattered was re-proven on the new substrate rather than carried over.
- `git describe` reaches back to `v0-legacy` on the archived tree; release
  builds match release-shaped tags only (found during `g02.022`).
